About WorldStitch

WorldStitch is an independent project built for people who build worlds. Whether you're running a tabletop campaign, writing a novel, or designing a game universe — your lore deserves a home that actually understands it. We're in early access, growing carefully, and taking applications from creators who want to shape what this becomes.
